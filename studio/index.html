Cover & monogram

The closed invitation, and how your monogram sits and moves on it.

What should the closed invitation show?

Your two-piece Canva monogram, or the built-in mark the site shipped with. Choosing the monogram replaces the old mark, its green circle and the word Open completely.

How solid is the closed cover?

How much the closed invitation hides the venue behind it. Lower lets more of the film show through before it is opened.

Cover frost

How blurred the venue looks through the closed cover.

Monogram size

Makes the complete monogram larger or smaller. Both pieces scale together and stay perfectly lined up.

Move monogram up / down

Moves both monogram pieces together, higher or lower on the closed invitation.

Move monogram left / right

Nudges both monogram pieces together, sideways.

Outer piece turns

Whether the outer part of the monogram slowly rotates while the invitation waits to be opened.

Rotation speed

How long the outer monogram takes to complete one full turn while waiting. Higher numbers are slower and calmer.

Rotation direction

Which way the outer monogram turns.

Centre piece breathes

Whether the centre of the monogram gently grows and settles while the invitation waits.

Breathing amount

How much the centre mark gently grows and settles. Deliberately small — this should be barely noticeable rather than a pulse.

Breathing speed

How long one gentle grow-and-settle takes. Higher numbers feel calmer.

Press response

How strongly the centre mark answers your tap, just before the invitation starts to open.

Monogram light

A subtle sense of light behind the monogram. Leave low — this is atmosphere, not a glow.

Entrance timing

How the invitation opens, and how long the venue gets on its own.

How long the cover takes to open

The physical movement of the cover clearing away from the invitation.

How long should we see just the venue?

The pause between the cover opening and the invitation beginning to appear. This is the moment the Ocean Pavilion film gets to itself.

How the invitation arrives

Whether the wording waits for the frosted material to develop, or the two arrive together.

Frost arrival speed

How quickly the invitation material develops over the venue, settling into the exact frost you have chosen below.

Invitation artwork arrival

How quickly the Canva wording resolves once it starts to appear.

Countdown & calendar delay

How long the countdown and Save to Calendar wait after the wording begins to appear. Zero brings them in together.

Video & frosted background

How much of the Ocean Pavilion film you can see, and how much of the phone the frosted invitation covers.

How much of the phone is frosted?

Makes the frosted invitation area taller or shorter. Making it shorter reveals more of the Ocean Pavilion video above and below at the same time. Phone layouts only — a computer keeps its invitation card.

Move frosted area up / down

Keeps the same frosted height but shifts the whole frosted area higher or lower, if you want more video at one end than the other.

Soften the frosted edges

How gently the frosted invitation blends back into the clearer video at the top and bottom. Low gives a defined edge; high gives a long, soft fade.

Invitation background strength

How solid the frosted invitation looks. Lower reveals more of the Ocean Pavilion film. Higher makes it cleaner and more paper-like.

Text background strength

The extra soft layer directly behind the wording. Lower lets more film show through the text. Higher makes the wording easier to read.

Frost strength

How blurred the venue looks through the frosted area. Lower feels clearer and more glass-like; higher feels softer. This is separate from how tall the frosted area is.

Background darkness

Makes the moving film behind the invitation lighter or darker. The video file itself is not changed.

Frosted-area height and position apply to phone layouts only. On a computer the invitation keeps its card.

⚠ Text may be difficult to read at this background setting.
