01 / The journey

A change of planes.
A change of pace.

Search for Cebu (CEB) — Mactan-Cebu International Airport. These are three sensible places to begin from London.

A good first search

01

Via Hong Kong

Cathay Pacific · London Heathrow

  1. LONLondon
  2. HKGHong Kong
  3. CEBCebu

Around 17 hourson shorter connections · one stop outbound

A straightforward route with one change in Hong Kong and a relatively short overall journey.

Connection notes

Look for London Heathrow to Cebu on one booking. Cathay’s route guide describes about 16½ hours; current shorter schedules are closer to 17 hours. Compare the connection time as well as the fare.

Singapore Airlines · London Heathrow or Gatwick

  1. LONLondon
  2. SINSingapore

Around 19–21 hourson shorter connections · one stop outbound

One change at Changi, with London airport options worth comparing for your journey.

Connection notes

Search from Heathrow and Gatwick separately. Representative schedules via Singapore are around 19–21 hours including the connection. Check the operating airline and baggage allowance on both legs, especially if a partner carrier is offered.

A different connection

03

Via Dubai

Emirates · London Heathrow

  1. LONLondon
  2. DXBDubai
  3. CEBCebu

Around 19 hourson shorter connections · one stop outbound

Worth comparing if the departure time or connection works better for you.

Connection notes

Shorter outbound schedules via Dubai are around 19 hours; longer connections can exceed 24. Check the return carefully: published Cebu services can include an additional stop before Dubai. Compare the whole return journey before choosing.
